# Exhibition Briefing Meeting - 2026-05-13

## Meeting Type

Full group meeting with academic staff about the dissertation exhibition.

## Summary

This meeting focused on exhibition requirements, key deadlines, installation responsibilities, safety, public communication, and how to present the dissertation project effectively. The exhibition is not assessed directly, but exhibition quality, visualisation, and communication are part of the dissertation assessment criteria. The main message was to plan the display early, communicate clearly with staff, and design the exhibition for a public audience.

## Key Points

### Purpose of the Exhibition

- The exhibition itself is not directly assessed.
- However, the dissertation assessment rubric includes marks for visualisation and communication, including exhibition-quality design.
- The exhibition is an opportunity to demonstrate the quality of the project, the skills developed during the course, and the ability to communicate a complex idea clearly.
- The aim is not simply to show research results.
- The exhibition should showcase what I have learned, what I have built, and why the project matters.
- The project should ideally be explainable in three clear sentences.

### Public Communication

- The audience will include members of the public, so the visual communication needs to be direct and understandable.
- The display should help visitors quickly understand what the project is, what problem it addresses, and why they should care.
- The project should tell a story rather than only present data or final results.
- The key issue, problem, or idea should be made visible through the display.

### Display Planning

- The display method should be considered early.
- It is better to raise display ideas early and discuss them with staff in advance.
- Staff can advise and support, but students are responsible for their own work and should not expect staff to do the project or installation for them.
- The exhibition space should be observed carefully, including lighting, layout, power access, circulation, safety, and environmental conditions.
- Safety issues must be considered, especially for physical prototypes, cables, screens, devices, fixings, or interactive elements.

### Installation Responsibilities

- Students are responsible for bringing all necessary equipment and installing their own work.
- All display tools, fixings, equipment, devices, and other materials must be brought to the exhibition site by the student.
- Students are responsible for their own objects and equipment.
- Screens must be booked or requested in advance if needed.
- If the project uses a QR code, link, or online resource, the exact code or link must be emailed to staff in advance.
