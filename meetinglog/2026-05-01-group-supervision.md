# Group Supervision Meeting - 2026-05-01

## Meeting Type

Group supervision meeting with the dissertation supervisor and other supervised students.

## Summary

This meeting focused on general dissertation planning, project management, ethical considerations, communication with supervisors, literature research, exhibition preparation, and the early direction of my proposed digital twin project. The main message was to stay organised, plan realistically, use available resources, and prepare both the dissertation work and exhibition output in a structured way.

## Key Points

### Dissertation Planning and Supervision

- Students may seek advice from any relevant academic staff member, not only their assigned supervisor, if their expertise is useful for the project.
- Ethical considerations should be reviewed early, especially if the project involves data, people, sensors, institutional systems, or public-facing outputs.
- The dissertation process should be carefully planned from the beginning, with attention to all important deadlines and milestones.
- If any materials need to be discussed in a supervision meeting, they should be emailed to the supervisor at least one day in advance so that there is enough time to review them.

### Time Management and Risk Planning

- A clear project timeline should be created, for example using a Gantt chart or another structured planning method.
- All major deadlines should be identified early.
- Internal deadlines can be moved one week earlier than the official deadlines to create a buffer.
- The project should include a Plan B in case the original plan cannot be completed.
- Work packages should be broken down into manageable sections, with alternative options prepared where possible.
- A fixed amount of working time should be protected on every working day.
- Being organised throughout the whole process is essential.

### Resources, Costs, and Backups

- Students should use all available university and course resources.
- Any project-related costs may be reimbursable if the item is necessary for the course or project, but reimbursed items may need to be returned.
- OneDrive can be used to store and track project progress.
- Everything should be backed up regularly, including writing, data, design files, code, and exhibition materials.

### Literature Review and Research Positioning

- Literature searching should continue throughout the project, especially within more specific sub-branches of the chosen topic.
- Similar or related projects can be compared to identify limitations, alternative methods, and opportunities for improvement.
- The literature review should not only describe existing work, but also help position the dissertation's contribution.

### Dissertation Writing Structure

The dissertation can begin with a working outline and be updated throughout the process. A possible 5,000-word structure is:

| Section | Approximate Word Count |
|---|---:|
| Introduction | 500 |
| Literature Review | 800 |
| Methodology | 1,000 |
| Data Collection and Analysis | 1,000 |
| Results and Discussion | 1,200 |
| Conclusion | 500 |

### Initial Project Direction

- I was considering a project related to digital twins.
- A project that only uses school MQTT data to display building data was considered too limited, because it would mainly visualise existing teaching building data without a stronger research contribution.
- The project needs a clearer research question, stronger analytical value, and a more meaningful output than simply displaying available data.

### Exhibition Considerations

- The exhibition needs to consider security, especially if the project involves live data, networks, devices, QR codes, or public interaction.
- It was suggested that visiting relevant design exhibitions, such as the new V&A museum exhibition, could help with thinking about public communication, display methods, and exhibition quality.

## Decisions

- I should develop a more focused digital twin project rather than simply visualising general building data from MQTT.
- I should prepare a structured project schedule with deadlines, work packages, and backup plans.
- I should begin drafting the dissertation outline early and update it continuously.
- I should continue searching for literature in more specific areas related to digital twins, data collection, equipment monitoring, and public-facing visualisation.
